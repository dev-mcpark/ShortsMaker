[Seoul's June 2010 development announcement](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/165130) independently names Hankuk Fiber as a bus-development partner and says replacing steel sheet with carbon composite reduced **body weight**, not total bus weight, by 25% to extend range. A [later Seoul account of the deployed route](https://mediahub.seoul.go.kr/archives/166241) places the bus on Namsan service. The local extractor returned 2,531 and 724 words for the first two articles, respectively, clearing its length gate without a model call.

## Unit-price paper story gate — 2026-10-13

The [Consumer Agency's June 13, 2024 release](https://www.kca.go.kr/home/sub.do?menukey=4002&mode=view&no=1003678922) links the [four-page original PDF](https://www.kca.go.kr/home/board/download.do?menukey=4002&fno=10042412&bid=00000013&did=1003678922). Its domestic-product appendix records one marinated garlic-stem product changing from 150g to 120g in March 2024. A footnote says its factory dispatch price was reduced from ₩4,490 to ₩3,590 during monitoring, yielding about ₩2,993 and ₩2,992 per 100g on May 9. These are **factory dispatch prices**, not observed retail prices, receipts, or proof that shoppers paid less. The release's 33-product finding covers its stated sources and observation period; it is not a current market prevalence estimate. No retail-price history was verified for this candidate.

**Five-beat text audition, unapproved:**

1. **Question:** “150g이던 반찬이 120g이 됐습니다. 그럼 100g당 가격도 20% 오른 걸까요?” Show two generic, clearly illustrative packages with their quantities, without a brand or copied packaging.
2. **Arithmetic:** “봉지 가격이 똑같이 3천 원이라면 100g당 2천 원에서 2천5백 원, 25% 오릅니다.” Label both ₩3,000 package prices as a **hypothetical example**. Animate the denominator change; typeset every number locally.
3. **Case turn:** “실제 한 반찬은 3월에 150g에서 120g으로 줄었습니다. 5월 조사 중엔 출고가격도 4,490원에서 3,590원으로 내려갔죠.” Attribute the 2024 case and label this second price layer **출고가격**. Do not turn it into a claim about the shop's price tag.
4. **Proof:** “100g당 출고가격은 약 2,993원에서 2,992원. 출고 단계의 단가는 거의 같아진 셈이죠.” Show the two `출고가격 ÷ 용량 × 100g` calculations side by side; the rounded figures do not establish a consumer saving.
5. **Payoff and limit:** “하지만 우리가 계산대에서 내는 판매가격은 따로 확인해야 합니다. 장볼 땐 가격표와 용량을 함께 보세요.” Show an intentionally unknown retail-price box, then a generic `판매가격 ÷ 용량` comparison. Do not show a fabricated old/new retail price.

The 25% hypothetical follows `3,000 ÷ 120` versus `3,000 ÷ 150`; the original PDF's rounded factory figures follow `4,490 ÷ 150 × 100` and `3,590 ÷ 120 × 100`. **Paper verdict:** the 20%-versus-25% arithmetic and dispatch-versus-retail twist can be shown with original diagrams for $0, but the topic's appeal is untested and the retail-price gap prevents a “what shoppers paid” claim.
